Standard ATS Compliance Guidelines

  • Keep formatting simple: Avoid multi-column layouts, tables, headers/footers, and embedded graphics that parser robots cannot read.
  • Use standard bullet characters: Stick to standard bullets (• or -) rather than special icons or emojis.
  • Clear contact block: Position your phone number, email address, and LinkedIn profile near the very top of your document.
  • Standard Section Names: Label your work history clearly as "Professional Experience" or "Work Experience" to ensure correct chronological parsing.
